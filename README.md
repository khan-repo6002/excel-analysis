# Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit grocery sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, product performance, and outlet-level insights. An interactive dashboard and PivotTable analysis are used to summarize key business metrics and present the findings in a clear, visual format.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that can help understand product demand, outlet performance, and sales distribution across different categories.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Compare sales across outlet sizes, outlet types, and location tiers.
- Understand sales distribution by item type and fat content.
- Evaluate sales trends based on outlet establishment year.
- Present business insights through an Excel dashboard.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Sales:** Overall sales generated across the dataset.
- **Number of Items:** Total item records analyzed.
- **Average Sales:** Average sales value per item record.
- **Average Rating:** Average customer rating represented in the dataset.

## Key Analysis Areas
- **Sales by Outlet Size:** Compare sales generated by small, medium, and high-sized outlets.
- **Sales by Fat Content:** Analyze sales for low-fat and regular products.
- **Sales by Item Type:** Identify product categories contributing to overall sales.
- **Sales by Outlet Type:** Compare sales performance across different outlet formats.
- **Sales by Establishment Year:** Examine sales distribution by outlet establishment year.
- **Sales by Location Tier:** Compare performance across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 locations.
- **Fat Content by Outlet Location:** Compare product fat-content sales across location tiers.

## Key Findings
Based on the workbook's summarized data:

- Total sales are approximately **1.20 million** in the dataset's reported sales units.
- The dataset contains **8,518 item records**.
- Average sales per item record are approximately **141.02**.
- Low-fat products contribute approximately **776K** in sales, compared with approximately **425K** for regular products.
- Medium-sized outlets account for approximately **508K** in sales, the highest among the three outlet-size categories.
- Tier 3 locations contribute approximately **472K** in sales, followed by Tier 2 and Tier 1 locations.
- Fruits and Vegetables and Snack Foods are among the leading item categories by sales.

*Note: These findings reflect the summarized figures in the workbook. Sales values are presented in the dataset's reported units.*
